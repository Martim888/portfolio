## Project Overview

ShoDrone is a modular software system developed as part of an academic project at the Instituto Superior de Engenharia do Porto (ISEP).

The system was designed to support the management, planning and simulation of drone shows, bringing together several business and technical components in a single solution.

The project covers customer management, drone fleet administration, show requests, commercial proposals and drone movement simulation.

## Main Features

### User and Customer Management
- User authentication and role-based access
- Customer registration and management
- Management of customer representatives

### Drone Fleet Management
- Registration and management of drone models
- Drone inventory administration
- Management of drone technical specifications

### Show Planning and Proposals
- Management of drone show requests
- Creation and management of commercial proposals
- Planning of drone performances

### Drone Simulation
- Simulation of drone movements
- Detection of possible collisions
- Concurrent programming and process synchronization

### Client-Server Communication
- TCP socket communication
- Exchange of information between system components

### Domain-Specific Languages
- Definition and processing of custom languages using ANTLR
- Interpretation of drone-related instructions

## Software Architecture

The project follows a layered architecture inspired by Domain-Driven Design (DDD), promoting separation of concerns, modularity and maintainability.

The system includes:

- **Presentation Layer:** Console applications and user interaction
- **Application Layer:** Controllers and application services
- **Domain Layer:** Business entities, rules and domain logic
- **Persistence Layer:** Repositories and database access
- **Infrastructure:** Networking, simulation and additional technical components
